The recurring and permanent modes, with entry conditions and the parts that matter. The big alliance-scale events (Alliance Expedition, Strongest Warzone, King of Ants) are on Alliance & PvP.

Crystal mine

Occupy veins with a squad to gather mystic crystals.

  • Vein markers: no marker = empty (no fight needed), green = yours, blue = alliance member (cannot raid), red = another player (raidable), purple = someone who raided you (revenge available)
  • What is in the squad affects neither mining speed nor total. The squad only defends the vein
  • Three tiers by yield and speed: crystal < rare crystal < superb crystal. The basic crystal mine allows one squad and no raiding; rare and superb allow up to four squads and raiding
  • A successful occupation grants 3 hours of protection
  • A successful raid takes 50% of what the target has mined and seizes the vein. A failed raid puts that vein on a 5-minute cooldown

Free occupation charges refill daily at 00:00 UTC. You start with 6 a day, +2 at Queen Lv25 and +2 when the amplification system unlocks. Diamonds can top you up to 10 a day, capped at 10 top-ups a week (reset Monday 00:00 UTC).

Topped-up charges that you do not spend that day are wiped at 00:00 UTC. Use them the day you buy them.

Special Ant Duel

The mode with the most unusual rules. There are no classes, only ranks.

  • You start at 1000 points. Winning takes points from the opponent, scaled by the gap between you
  • Tickets cap at 10 held, with 5 free challenges a day. Diamonds buy more, but each purchase costs more than the last
  • While you are attacking, or being attacked, nobody else can challenge you
  • Battle history holds 30 entries. You may strike back at someone who beat you once per entry (not if they only defended successfully)
  • Your defence squad cannot be left empty — every squad needs at least one special ant. You may hide one squad from other players
  • Dismantling a special ant or neutral creature that sits in your defence squad removes it from that squad when you are next attacked

The key point: awakening, medals, conqueror evolution and worker treasure buffs are all disabled here (see Growth Systems). A strong duel line-up is not the same as a strong field line-up.

Trial of Courage (pangolin)

Runs 3 days. Requires Queen Lv12+.

  • R4/R5 use bait to summon the pangolin; only alliance members can attack it, solo or by rally
  • 15 attacks per day
  • The pangolin cannot be killed. Score is damage dealt
  • Its stats change daily — follow the hint and rebuild around the recommended soldier type
  • Four reward tracks: personal tier, personal ranking, alliance tier, alliance ranking

This is where reform chests for troop reform come from.

Flying Lizard Battle (rainbow lizard)

Runs 6 days. Requires Queen Lv14+, and your server must have run a season and not be in one now.

  • One lizard spawns daily at 10:00. Rallies are not allowed — solo attacks only
  • 5 attacks a day, each costing 5 stamina
  • March time is capped at 30 seconds for these attacks
  • The lizard cannot be killed; score is damage

The recommended soldier type rotates by weekday.

Day Recommended
Mon / Thu Guardian
Tue / Fri Shooter
Wed / Sat Carrier

Alliance Trial (frilled lizard)

Cycles through preparation (bait placement, 48 hours) → attackable → attack.

  • Only members who have not joined an Alliance Trial in the last 46 hours can take part
  • Players who entered a season map or the expedition rainforest map, or who left a season map less than 46 hours ago, are excluded
  • Soldiers take neither severe wounds nor deaths here — only light wounds, which heal automatically afterwards
  • R4/R5 place the bait on the field map, and the alliance can schedule the trial's start time

Migration Journey

  • You can only migrate within your warzone group; each warzone accepts at most 300 players
  • Each warzone's King of Ants can set a maximum anthill level for incoming players
  • If your power would place you near the top of the destination, the destination's King must approve you
  • Migration items required scale with anthill level:

Event progress can reset when you move warzones; claimable rewards arrive by mail.

Warzone Summons

During the season sign-up window, a leader can invite unaffiliated players from other warzones. To start one, the alliance needs:

  • 30+ active members over the last three days
  • 25+ members who unlocked T8 soldiers logging in over the last three days
  • Total alliance evolution of 1200+

Summons from alliances that fall below these are cancelled by the system daily between 23:55 and 00:05 UTC. A summons cannot be withdrawn once started — only its conditions edited.

Season hatching

Opens at 00:00 UTC on the Thursday of the week the season match stage begins. One season mutation egg per pull.

Luck points are the mechanism — one point per pull.

Luck Reward
100 A random ant from part of the season pool
300 A random ant from the entire season pool
600 / 800 A season hatching phantom mutation egg

Ants obtained through luck points do not count toward the "guaranteed season ant every 30 pulls" counter, and luck resets to 0 when the season ends.

Season mutation eggs do not carry over. Unused ones become ordinary supreme mutation eggs when the season ends, and leftover fragments expire into a small amount of diamonds. Spend them.
