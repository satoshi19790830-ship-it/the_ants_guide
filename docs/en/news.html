S6 Mutated Barren Season Major Adjustment Announcement

2026-09-05

A large set of season changes, applied to events whose sign-up period starts on or after September 7.

Fruit Scramble

  • War declarations are limited to a 4-hour window before the Protection Period, and active declarations are cancelled when battle time ends
  • The war declaration preparation period is cut to 10 minutes
  • Each alliance may declare war on only one fortress, and each camp may target only one enemy camp fortress
  • Camp outposts gain permanent protection and a wider protected area
  • Fortresses no longer recover durability while troops are stationed inside
  • Troops stationed in your own camp fortress get their stamina back if they never fought

New: Truce Day

For two weeks after Champion Points Stage II begins, Wednesdays and Saturdays (UTC) are truce days. Attacking fortresses and building alliance towers are both blocked. Season stamina, revival fungi and alliance clay do not recover, but a limited-time Colony Treasure Hunt opens instead.

Auto-join rallies (all seasons)

Troops now join a rally within 1 minute 30 seconds at most when the distance requirement is met, with a 10-second delay so manual joiners take priority. Joining inside war declaration areas was also improved.

Map terrain and fortress positions were rebalanced for fairness.

[The Ants] Aug 17 Scheduled Maintenance Notice

2026-08-17

New

  • Three awakening parts added to the Maze Store (Cyphomyrmex Rimosus, Saharan Silver Ant, Meranoplus Castaneus)

Adjustments

  • The healing screen now auto-selects the maximum number of soldier ants your resources can heal
  • Insect Buddy Gallery level cap raised
  • Anthill skin and alliance battle screen effects improved
  • Text descriptions improved across several interfaces

Fixes

  • Hunting Ground contract quantities could not be changed
  • Fast healing button display issues during seasonal wild activities
  • Abnormal alliance expedition registration notifications
  • Several Mutated Barren beta issues (sentinel tree alerts, troop positioning in battle, fortress colours on the map, anthill teleportation, fortress battle page display, battle loss report figures)

[The Ants] August 3 Scheduled Maintenance Notice

2026-08-03

Maintenance window 07:00–08:00 UTC.

New

  • Tabs for viewing plunder and protection logs in the Colony Treasure Hunt
  • An "All Soldier Types" tab on the Soldiers Reform page
  • Quick Open for reform chests (up to 999 at once)

Adjustments

  • Lower development resource costs for the Mutation System, Soldiers Reform, Amp System and Special Ant Evolution
  • Occupied fortresses in certain areas no longer give Fruitful Points
  • Lower Fruitful Point requirements for tier rewards
  • Lower queen level requirement to access the Colony Treasure Hunt
  • The Cancel War Declaration button was moved
  • Reform Cooperation unlock requirements changed

Fixes

  • Fiddler crabs could not be attacked normally
  • Abnormal camp protection countdown display during the season
