Tutorial

The tutorial runs through chapter 4. If you just follow the prompts you won't get stuck. By the end, your queen ant should be around level 8.

Priority #1: Level up your queen ant

The Ants is heavily a "growth" game — leveling up your queen ant is the top priority. Your anthill level equals your queen's level, and almost every building is capped by it (even buildings bought from the store). Getting her to around level 25 makes the rest of the game noticeably easier.

Other key milestones

Level What happens
Queen 8 New-player shield auto-expires (you become raidable)
Queen 16 Neutral creature upgrades unlock
Soldier barracks 22 T8 soldier ants unlock
Queen/barracks 23 Golden eggs (1) available from zone wars
Queen/barracks 25 T9 soldier ants unlock, golden eggs (3) available from zone wars

The queen-level-8 shield expiration is especially important to plan for — have your defenses (alliance membership, resource spending) ready before it happens.

Playstyle trait system

Unlocked at queen level 7, "playstyle traits" let you pick a colony trait from three lines: Cultivation, Plunder, or Pastoral. Each has a dedicated building and exactly 3 clear pros and 3 clear cons (currently switchable for free).

Trait Dedicated building Pros Cons
Cultivation Resource Processing Plant Resource protection (harder to raid) / +50% gathering speed / +100% troop carrying capacity while gathering -30% ATK while invading / -30% DEF while invading / -30% march speed
Plunder Loot Warehouse +150% troop carrying capacity while invading / 40% of would-be deaths convert to wounded / +100% wounded-troop healing speed -30% plant/meat/wet-soil/sand output / -30% honeydew output / -50% troop carrying capacity while gathering
Pastoral Resource Transport Passage +50% honeydew output / -30% troop food consumption / -5% prices at the Traveling Insect Habitat +100% resources lost to raids while invaded / -50% wounded-troop healing speed (a possible 3rd con unconfirmed)

Source: our own account's playstyle-trait selection screen (measured 2026-07-19; Cultivation and Plunder rows measured that day, Pastoral row confirmed 2026-07-26)

About rerolling

Rerolling is generally not recommended:

  • The reroll process itself is tedious
  • Normal progression already gives you strong ants fairly easily

Joining an alliance

Joining an alliance clearly speeds up your nest's development, so it's worth doing early. See Alliance & PvP for more.

Free-to-play viability

Daily login bonuses and daily tasks are generous, so free-to-play players can absolutely get strong. Don't skip your dailies.

Common early mistakes to avoid

  • Stockpiling resources above the "protected" threshold, making yourself a raid target
  • Letting your shield expire without defenses in place
  • Skipping recurring events (dailies, alliance expeditions) and missing out on steady rewards
