Gamera Collaboration

Last updated: 2026-09-06
On this page
  • Dates
  • What it adds
  • Explore the Mysterious Continent
  • The teaser story that led up to it
  • What I still can't confirm
  • Sources

The Ants × Gamera launched on August 31, 2026. The official framing is that the legendary guardian monster Gamera and the Ants expedition team go on an adventure across worlds.

This page is a dated record of what the developers actually announced. Anything I could not confirm is marked as unconfirmed. I will fill the gaps in as I can check them in game.

Dates

What When
Collaboration starts August 31, 2026
Explore the Mysterious Continent August 31 - September 6, 2026
Forum participation campaign until September 3, 2026, 23:59:59 UTC

The end date of the collaboration as a whole is not stated in the announcements I could find (unconfirmed).

What it adds

The rewards named in the official announcement are:

  • Selective Special Ant
  • Collaboration Insect Skin
  • Selective Exquisite Rare Special Ant Fragment Chest

Whether a brand-new collaboration special ant was added, or the "selective" chest simply picks from existing ants, is not clear from the announcement text (unconfirmed). How Gamera itself appears in game — as a neutral creature, as a skin, or only in artwork — is likewise unconfirmed.

Explore the Mysterious Continent

This is the core activity of the collaboration.

  • Dig tiles on an unknown continent to uncover buried rewards
  • Some tiles glow, and digging those can trigger an extra event
  • Digging turns up items such as stone tablets

In the forum comments, players reported finding three to five stone tablets, and running into fierce creatures while digging.

The teaser story that led up to it

The collaboration did not appear out of nowhere. From mid-August the official forum dripped out a story. Lined up, it reads clearly:

Date Post What happens
Aug 17 Unknown Event #2: A Rift in Space A rift opens in space
Aug 17 Unknown Event #3: The Exploration Team An expedition team is formed
Aug 20 Unknown Event #4: A Mysterious Power A strange power is detected
Aug 23 Unknown Event #5: The Slumbering Ancient Tree An ancient tree wakes, and something is roused
Aug 26 The Truth Revealed: The Guardian Monster Awakens The guardian monster is revealed as Gamera
Aug 28 The Ants × Gamera - 3 days to go Countdown
Aug 31 Collaboration begins Explore the Mysterious Continent opens

Rift, then expedition, then a strange power, then the ancient tree, then the reveal. Note that the August 17-26 posts were forum participation campaigns (comment with your game ID for a chance at rewards), not in-game events.

What I still can't confirm

To be straight about it, a lot about this collaboration is still open:

  • The end date of the collaboration as a whole
  • Whether the collaboration special ant is newly implemented or picked from existing ants
  • How Gamera appears in game
  • What the insect skin actually looks like, and how it is obtained

I checked the official Japanese update notice for August 31 (【8月31日】アップデートのお知らせ) and it does not mention the collaboration at all — it covers evolution, the underground cave and Pioneer optimisations plus bug fixes. The collaboration is announced separately from the patch notes. I will fill this page in once I can check it in game. If you know the answers, please tell me.
