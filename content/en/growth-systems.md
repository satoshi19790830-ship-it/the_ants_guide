The Ants stacks **many systems onto the same stat, each adding a little**. Opening the breakdown tooltip on my own account showed "Damage amplification: 66.00%" resolving to evolution 20.00% + neutral creatures 5.00% + buildings 41.00% (see finding 4 on [Verifying the Damage Formula](damage-calculation.html)). Rather than maxing one thing, **the win is not leaving any of them empty**.

This page is the map of what feeds what.

## Overview

| System | Unlock | Mainly raises | Special Ant Duel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bacteria | Evolution Fungi Lv5 + Queen Lv17–20 | Per-soldier-type ATK / DEF / HP | — |
| Genes | Build the Mutation Fungi | Per-soldier-type ATK / DEF / HP | — |
| Cells | Build the Mutation Fungi | Per-soldier-type ATK / DEF / HP | — |
| Fungi | Build the Mutation Fungi | All soldier ATK/DEF/HP + special ant skill ATK/DEF | — |
| Troop reform | Troop reform pool level | Per-soldier-type stats | — |
| Neutral creature fossils & gems | Queen Lv16 + server age | Front / middle / back row stats | partly |
| Worker treasures | Treasure system unlock | Various buffs | **disabled** |
| Medals | Queen Lv19 | Various buffs | **disabled** |
| Special ant awakening | Own one awakening part | Row buffs + awakening skills | **disabled** |
| Conqueror evolution | Evolution slot growth | Stats + an evolution skill | **disabled** |
| VIP | Login / diamonds | Various buffs, VIP shop | — |
| Skin star-up | Unlocking skins | A stat buff per star | — |

> **Cross-cutting rule:** awakening, medals, conqueror evolution and worker treasures are all stated in the official help to be **disabled in the Special Ant Duel**. Duel rankings therefore reflect accounts without any of them. The one exception is the neutral creature fossil set bonus for "deployed soldier count", which does apply in the duel.

## Mutation Fungi: bacteria, genes, cells, fungi

All four open once you build the Mutation Fungi, and all four are **built the same way**, so learning one teaches the rest.

- Parts have **9 star ranks (2★–10★)**; cells cap at 7★
- Enhance to raise the level, star up to raise the cap, break through to go further
- You can hold duplicates, but **not two of the same name unlocked in the same slot**
- Feeding an enhanced part back in returns its full accumulated experience — nothing is wasted

**Bacteria** — 3 slots per colony, up to 4 colonies. Colonies unlock with Evolution Fungi Lv5 plus Queen **Lv17 / Lv18 / Lv19 / Lv20**.

**Genes** — the four soldier parts (mandible, antenna, carapace, abdomen), 3 gene fragments each.

| Gene | Effect |
|---|---|
| Strike | Guardian ATK / Shooter DEF / Carrier HP |
| Venom | Shooter ATK / Carrier DEF / Guardian HP |
| Armour | Carrier ATK / Guardian DEF / Shooter HP |

Enhancing costs meat, sand and water; starring up costs DNA.

**Cells** — six types, max 7★, each covering a different rotation of ATK / DEF / HP across the three soldier types. Enhancing costs cell fluid and honeydew.

**Fungi** — four fungal colonies (filamentous, algal, oval, cystic). The colonies themselves raise **all soldiers' ATK, DEF and HP**. The fungi placed inside follow the same three-way rotation, except each colony's **"nucleus dish" fungus, which raises special ant skill ATK and skill DEF** — the only one that feeds special ant skill damage directly.

## Troop reform

Each of the three soldier types has **five normal slots (three troop-reform, two universal) and two rare slots**.

- Troop-reform and rare slots unlock by **troop reform pool level**; universal slots unlock via that type's **reform cooperation** rank
- Parts are typed: troop-reform parts fit only their type's slots, all-type parts fit all-type slots, universal parts fit any universal slot
- Upgrading costs resources, duplicates and reform medium. Parts come from **reform chests**, obtained from the Trial of Courage, events and packs

The August 3, 2026 update cut the resource cost, and **players who had already invested were refunded the difference**.

## Worker treasures

Once the treasure system opens, worker squads **split into build squads and exploration squads, and the same squad can do both at once**. Exploration yields treasure fragments; unlocking a treasure grants a buff.

- Quality runs blue < purple < orange
- Purple and orange only appear once the exploration squad reaches a certain level
- "Island treasures" unlock after the cave treasure **Mystic Fossil reaches Lv.15**
- The purple material "treasure clay" is capped at **180 per week**

## Medals

Unlock at Queen **Lv19**. Completing tasks unlocks medals that grant extra stats.

- Extra stats from the same medal type **do not stack** — only your highest currently unlocked one applies
- **They work whether or not you equip them** (you can display up to three)
- Active in every battle except the Special Ant Duel

## VIP

VIP has **15 levels**. Daily login gives free VIP points, and they can be bought with diamonds. The daily amount grows with your login streak, and **missing a day does not reset it**.

The VIP shop runs as an event **every Saturday to Sunday**; higher VIP levels unlock more items.

## Skins and star-up

Anthill, march squad, chat bubble and photo frame skins exist; anthill and squad skins also have **star-up, from 0 to 10 stars**.

- Each star unlocks a new stat buff
- **The skin's own buff and the star-up buff are calculated separately and both apply**
- Limited-time skins give no star-up experience, and their buffs last only while valid. Note that the clock **starts when you unlock them, whether or not you use them**

## Neutral creature growth

Beyond levelling the creature itself, there are **fossils, gems and gem talents**, **rank-up talents and breakthroughs**, and **companions**.

Fossils fill 6 slots in 3 groups, and each group buffs the **front, middle or back row**. Each fossil takes 3 gems, which map to the same rows.

| Fossil | Effect |
|---|---|
| Venom gland | Front row ATK |
| Carapace | Front row DEF |
| Lepidopteran | Middle row ATK |
| Raised abdomen | Middle row DEF |
| Hunter | Back row ATK |
| Great pincer | Back row DEF |

Gems are Hard-shell Emerald (front), Strong-jaw Sapphire (middle) and Sharp-claw Topaz (back), each covering ATK, DEF and HP. **Once equipped, fossils and gems cannot be removed or swapped** — only enhanced.

Fossils unlock at Queen **Lv16** plus a server-age requirement; the gem system opens once any fossil reaches Rare quality. Each gem enhancement grants one talent point.

**Companion bonds** need purple-or-better companions, unlock when all members hit 5★, and reach a second tier at 8★. Current bonds: Gold Wing, Feast of Petals, and Dance of Butterflies.

## Special ant growth

Awakening is covered on [Ant Types & Stats](ant-types.html). The other layers:

- **Evolution amplification (deploying special ants)** — only **rare** special ants can be deployed, and higher generations amplify more. What you get depends **only on how many you deploy and their generation**, not which evolution set they go into
- **Special ant deployment** — six slots per special ant evolution, filled **by same-season group only** (no individual placement). The bonus steps up at 2, 4 and 6 owned; under 2 of a season, you cannot deploy that season at all
- **Skill breakthrough** — S5-season ants and later can break through **skill 1**, consuming one of the ant's own fragments. The breakthrough stat is fixed regardless of skill 1's level. Rolling back is free, has no cooldown, and can be done while the ant is idle
- **Conqueror evolution** — six slots; when all reach the current cap you can break through. Quality runs white → green → blue → purple → orange → red. Once all six have enough breakthroughs you gain an **evolution skill**, one per squad
