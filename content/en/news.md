## 2026-09

### [S6 Mutated Barren Season Major Adjustment Announcement](https://theantsforum.allstarunion.com/t/s6-mutated-barren-season-major-adjustment-announcement/56398)

2026-09-05

A large set of season changes, applied to events whose sign-up period starts on or after September 7.

**Fruit Scramble**

- War declarations are limited to a 4-hour window before the Protection Period, and active declarations are cancelled when battle time ends
- The war declaration preparation period is cut to 10 minutes
- Each alliance may declare war on only one fortress, and each camp may target only one enemy camp fortress
- Camp outposts gain permanent protection and a wider protected area
- Fortresses no longer recover durability while troops are stationed inside
- Troops stationed in your own camp fortress get their stamina back if they never fought

**New: Truce Day**

For two weeks after Champion Points Stage II begins, Wednesdays and Saturdays (UTC) are truce days. Attacking fortresses and building alliance towers are both blocked. Season stamina, revival fungi and alliance clay do not recover, but a limited-time Colony Treasure Hunt opens instead.

**Auto-join rallies (all seasons)**

Troops now join a rally within 1 minute 30 seconds at most when the distance requirement is met, with a 10-second delay so manual joiners take priority. Joining inside war declaration areas was also improved.

Map terrain and fortress positions were rebalanced for fairness.
